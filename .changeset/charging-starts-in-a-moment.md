**Switching the limit off says charging is about to start.** On Macs that limit through macOS's own charge limit, macOS can take up to a minute to start charging again after the limit is released. The panel used to say "Plugged in, not charging" for that whole time, as if the switch had done nothing. It now says "Plugged in", with "Charging starts in a moment" (the same message shown while eWiz settles after wake), until current starts flowing.
