**⌘Q no longer quits eWiz by accident.** Clicking the panel or one of eWiz's windows makes
eWiz the active app, so a ⌘Q meant for the app you were just using quit eWiz instead. It
disappeared from the menu bar without a word, and hotkeys, automations and keep-awake
stopped until you opened it again. ⌘Q now does nothing in eWiz; the power button in the
panel is how you quit.
