**The icon, the panel and the notifications now agree.** They all read one shared
account of what the battery is doing, so the menu bar can no longer show a full battery
with a check mark while the panel says 49% and charging. The "charge complete" flash
fires only when the battery actually reaches full or your limit after charging, never
when "Don't charge" is switched on part-way, and it always ends after three seconds.
"Charge limit reached" is only announced when the limit is actually reached.
