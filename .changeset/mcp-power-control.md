**AI agents can now change power settings when you ask.** Through eWiz's MCP server, Claude and other agents can turn Low Power Mode on or off, switch the eWiz mode, set the charge limit, pause or resume charging, and change sleep settings like Power Nap. `ewiz_status` now also reports the charge limit, mode, sleep settings and temperature. A new switch in Settings › AI Agents turns this off.
