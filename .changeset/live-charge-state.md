**The menu bar and panel follow the cable and charging as they change.** eWiz now listens to the battery itself, so current starting after a resume or a plug-in, and pulling the cable while a hold has the adapter cut, both show up in a second instead of on the next poll. A timed pause leaves "Charging paused" the moment it runs out. A slider closed mid-drag can no longer freeze the panel's status until relaunch, and an older status reply can no longer overwrite a newer one. The helper also reacts when charging starts or stops, not only when the power source switches.
