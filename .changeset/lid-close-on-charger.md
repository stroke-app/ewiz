**A close on the charger no longer reads as a perfect night.** eWiz now records whether the Mac was plugged in when the lid closed and opened. A close that charged shows the gain instead of "no drop", and the Sealed Sleep result only counts closes on battery, so "51% → 80%, no drop" can't pass for zero drain. The "Never" hibernate option now says what it costs: about 3% a day.
