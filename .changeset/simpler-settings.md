**Settings are easier to find your way around.** The seven tabs across the top are now a sidebar grouped into Battery, Power and eWiz. Each pane starts with one plain sentence about what it's for and a link to a page on ewiz.app that explains every setting. Settings most people never need (sleep enforcement, discharge, the MagSafe LED, charge power, AI agents, network profiles and the like) are hidden until you turn on **Advanced settings**, so a new Mac owner first sees the handful that matter. Some groups have clearer names, for example "Battery health" instead of "Long-term care" and "Heat protection" instead of "Heat". **Donate** now goes straight to the pay-what-you-want checkout.
