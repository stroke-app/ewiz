**The menu bar switches to charging promptly.** After a plug-in, eWiz asked the helper for its status only twice, both before the helper had reacted, and never again once current actually started flowing, so "Plugged in" or "Held" could stay up for most of a minute. It now asks again over the next ten seconds, and also when the charger takes over and when charging starts or stops.
