**The helper reacts the moment you plug in.** It used to notice a plug or unplug only on its next check, up to 10 seconds later, or a full minute after the lid had been closed on battery. Until then the charge limit, the hold and the status the menu shows all trailed the cable. The helper now wakes on every power-source change. This updates the helper, which installs itself without a password.
