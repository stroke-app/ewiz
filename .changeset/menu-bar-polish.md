**Menu bar and panel fixes.** A hold that cuts the adapter no longer plays the unplug and plug-in sounds or the overlay, and the icon and gauge keep their on-power colour while it holds. The gauge's marker shows where charging actually stops (macOS's step, or the hold's level), the "Resumes in" and Awake countdowns tick while the panel is open, and the panel no longer redraws every five seconds when nothing changed. The status item has a VoiceOver label and tooltip, and turning on Reduce Motion takes effect at once, including the gauge's pulse.
